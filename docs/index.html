Client-side demo

CAPRA AI Privacy Risk Assessor

This static demo runs the CAPRA privacy-risk scoring workflow entirely in your browser using the same taxonomy and assessment logic as the Python CLI/API/MCP tool. It is grounded in the published taxonomies Privacy in the Age of AI: A Taxonomy of Data Risks and A Taxonomy of Data Risks in AI and Quantum Computing (QAI) — A Systematic Review.

Getting started

How to use this tool

  1. Enter a system name. A short label for whatever you're assessing (e.g. "Customer support chatbot" or "Internal HR AI assistant").
  2. Rate each risk below. The risks are grouped by category, from both source taxonomies. For every risk, choose one option from its dropdown:
    • Present — this risk exists in your system today.
    • Not present — you've checked, and this risk doesn't apply / is mitigated.
    • Not applicable — this risk category is out of scope for your system.
    • Unknown — you haven't investigated this yet.
    You don't have to rate every risk. Anything left as — Not rated — is treated the same as "unknown" and listed under Unrated risks in your report, so you can see at a glance what still needs investigating.
  3. Click "Assess". Your report is generated instantly, entirely in your browser — nothing you enter is sent to a server. The page loads a static copy of the taxonomy once, then scores everything locally in JavaScript.
  4. Review your report. You'll get an overall score, a breakdown by category, any risks flagged as high-priority (the most frequently reported risks in the underlying research that you marked as present), plain-language recommendations, and the list of unrated risks to follow up on.

Under the hood

Same engine as the CLI/API/MCP tool

This page runs the identical scoring logic as the Python CLI/API/MCP tool, ported to JavaScript and tested against it for exact parity. It's grounded in the published taxonomies Privacy in the Age of AI: A Taxonomy of Data Risks and A Taxonomy of Data Risks in AI and Quantum Computing (QAI) — A Systematic Review.

Your report will include

  • Overall score and per-category scores
  • Flagged high-risk items and related cross references
  • Recommendation strings generated from the source definitions
  • Count and list of unrated risks
